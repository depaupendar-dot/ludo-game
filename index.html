How to Play

  • Roll 6 to bring a token onto the board.
  • Move according to the dice number.
  • Rolling 6 gives another turn.
  • Three consecutive 6s lose the turn.
  • ⭐ Safe squares cannot be captured.
  • Capture opponents by landing on them.
  • Reach the center with an exact roll.
  • First player with all 4 tokens finished wins.
